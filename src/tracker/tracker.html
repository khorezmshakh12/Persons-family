Welcome to Quiet Progress Operating System

This is your complete productivity operating system. Every part is organically connected:

Track daily micro-habits. Your completion percentages, smooth curve graphs, live streak flame counters (🔥), and the right-hand Analysis panel update automatically.

Connects habits, tasks, and mindset into an executive overview with a 31-day activity heatmap and discipline index.

Switch between January and December at the bottom. Every single month maintains its own persistent data, and full Undo/Redo (Ctrl+Z) is active at all times.
